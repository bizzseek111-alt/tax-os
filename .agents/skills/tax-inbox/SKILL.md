# Tax Inbox Skill

## 1. Overview
Replaces traditional tax questionnaires with an intelligent, card-based inbox of unresolved tax facts.

## 2. Core Responsibilities
Surfaces only high-leverage questions that directly impact tax liability or legal eligibility, enabling one-click resolution and instant refund updates.

## 3. Principles & Invariants
- Target WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility standards.
- Never show raw unverified AI output as definitive tax authority.
- Maintain high visual trust, clear typography, and tabular numeric alignment.
- Prioritize user agency, explainability, and progressive disclosure at all times.
