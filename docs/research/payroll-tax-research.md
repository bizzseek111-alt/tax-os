# Payroll & Employment Tax Legal Authority Research

## 1. Domain Overview

Payroll and employment tax calculations are governed by federal and state statutory withholding rules, contribution caps, and strict administrative deposit schedules.

The `PayrollAuthoritySpecialist` conducts authoritative research in the `ResearchDomain.PAYROLL_TAX` domain.

## 2. Core Federal Statutory Framework

| Provision | Code / Citation | Rule Summary |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **FICA (Social Security)** | 26 U.S.C. §§ 3101(a), 3111(a) | 6.2% employee + 6.2% employer on wages up to statutory wage base cap ($176,100). |
| **FICA (Medicare)** | 26 U.S.C. §§ 3101(b), 3111(b) | 1.45% employee + 1.45% employer on all wages (no cap). |
| **Additional Medicare** | 26 U.S.C. § 3101(b)(2) | 0.9% employee withholding on wages exceeding $200,000 ($250,000 MFJ). |
| **FUTA** | 26 U.S.C. §§ 3301, 3302 | 6.0% on first $7,000; up to 5.4% state credit = 0.6% net rate ($42/employee). |
| **Deposit Schedules** | 26 CFR § 31.6302-1 | Lookback period liability $\le \$50,000$ = Monthly; $> \$50,000$ = Semi-Weekly; $\ge \$100,000$ in one day = Next-day deposit mandatory. |

## 3. Worker Classification Analysis (1099 vs W-2)

Worker classification disputes trigger severe liability under IRC § 3509 for back withholding, employer FICA, and penalties.

TaxOS conducts statutory classification analysis using:
1. **20-Factor Common Law Test (Rev. Rul. 87-41)** across three statutory pillars:
   - Behavioral Control (instructions, training, mandatory hours).
   - Financial Control (significant investment, unreimbursed expenses, opportunity for profit/loss).
   - Type of Relationship (written contracts, employee benefits, permanence).
2. **Section 530 Relief (Revenue Act of 1978)**: Safe harbor defense based on reasonable basis, judicial precedent, or long-standing industry practice.
