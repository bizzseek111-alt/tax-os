# Sales & Use Tax Legal Authority Research

## 1. Domain Overview

Sales and use tax is governed by statutory economic nexus standards established under *South Dakota v. Wayfair, Inc.*, 138 S. Ct. 2080 (2018), local sourcing rules, product/service taxability matrices, and marketplace facilitator laws.

The `SalesTaxAuthoritySpecialist` conducts authoritative research in the `ResearchDomain.SALES_TAX` domain.

## 3. Product & Service Taxability Research (SaaS & Digital Goods)

- **New York:** SaaS is classified as prewritten computer software transferred electronically and is **SUBJECT TO SALES TAX** (NY Tax Law § 1101(b)(6), TSB-M-08(7)S).
- **California:** Pure SaaS accessed remotely where no tangible media or software code is transferred to customer possession is **EXEMPT** from California sales tax (18 CCR § 1502).
- **New Jersey:** Pure cloud-hosted SaaS without software download is generally **EXEMPT** from sales tax.

## 4. Marketplace Facilitator Relief

Under modern state facilitator statutes, marketplace facilitators (Amazon, Shopify, Walmart) bear statutory legal liability for collecting and remitting sales tax on third-party marketplace sales. Remote sellers are relieved of remittance burdens for marketplace sales, but marketplace revenue counts toward economic nexus thresholds in California and New York.
