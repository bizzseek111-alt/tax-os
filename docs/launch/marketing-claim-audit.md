# TaxOS Marketing Claims & Representation Audit
**Document Version:** 1.0.0  
**Phase:** 10 Launch Hardening  
**Target Environment:** Public Website, Documentation & Sales Materials  
**Audit Standard:** FTC Guidelines on Truth in Advertising, AICPA Ethics Rules, Circular 230 Standards  

---

## 1. Audit Rationale & Regulatory Context

In financial and tax compliance software, overstated claims create profound legal and regulatory liabilities. Exaggerated statements such as "100% automated," "zero hallucinations," or "fully autonomous tax preparation" violate FTC truth-in-advertising guidelines and Circular 230 standards governing tax professionals.

This audit reviews all public and client-facing representations, explicitly prohibiting deceptive superlatives and replacing them with rigorous, defensible technical statements.

---

## 2. Prohibited vs. Approved Statements Audit Table

| Topic / Category | Prohibited / Deceptive Claim | Approved / Factual Representation | Technical Justification |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Automation** | *"TaxOS provides 100% fully autonomous end-to-end filing with no human needed."* | *"TaxOS combines deterministic calculation engines and multi-agent intelligence with mandatory human review workflows for licensed professionals."* | The filing state machine strictly prevents automated return submission; taxpayer authorization and professional sign-off are mandatory. |
| **Accuracy** | *"TaxOS guarantees 100% tax accuracy with zero errors on any tax return."* | *"TaxOS executes deterministic, zero-floating-point calculations using 64-bit integer cents across tested federal and state statutory rules."* | Math calculations are deterministic, but final accuracy depends on taxpayer facts and professional judgment. |
| **AI Reliability** | *"Our AI models never hallucinate tax laws or statutory citations."* | *"TaxOS implements an authoritative Tax Authority Engine with automated citation validation that refuses hallucinated or non-existent legal codes."* | AI agents can propose citations, but the deterministic validator rejects any that do not exist in canonical law. |
| **Audit Immunity** | *"Using TaxOS makes your business 100% audit-proof against the IRS."* | *"TaxOS generates an immutable cryptographic Evidence Graph and detailed statutory calculation lineage to support professional review and documentation standards."* | No software can guarantee that a taxing authority will not audit a taxpayer. |
| **Speed / Turnaround** | *"File your multi-state corporate taxes in under 30 seconds."* | *"TaxOS automates ingestion, data extraction, and preliminary schedule preparation, accelerating review turnaround for credentialed preparers."* | Professional review and reconciliation require thorough review. |
| **Filing Coverage** | *"TaxOS supports all U.S. federal and state taxes."* | *"TaxOS currently supports Federal individual returns (Form 1040), standard schedules, and five state income tax modules (CA, NY, NJ, IL, MA), alongside sales and payroll engines."* | Only 5 states and standard forms are in the active supported scope. |

## 3. Disclaimers & Regulatory Notices

All TaxOS user interfaces and generated reports must display the following standardized regulatory disclaimer:

> **Important Regulatory Notice:**  
> *TaxOS is a tax preparation and compliance productivity platform for taxpayers and credentialed tax professionals. Tax calculations and legal research generated by TaxOS are intended to assist, not replace, the independent professional judgment of a Certified Public Accountant, Enrolled Agent, or Tax Attorney. Electronic filing requires explicit taxpayer authorization under IRS Form 8879.*

1. All marketing collateral, website copy, sales decks, and repository README files must be audited against this matrix.
2. Any copy utilizing prohibited superlatives must be revised immediately before Private Beta customer onboarding.
